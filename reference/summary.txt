RL4EQ reference summary
更新日期：2026-07-31

研究路线：
Level B 极端稀疏长回波信道 + 整帧缓冲、非因果块均衡器 + Pilot 条件监督预训练 + 在线按帧持续适配 + Continual PPO 控制器。

一、神经接收机与在线学习

1. DeepRx: Fully Convolutional Deep Learning Receiver
   文件：DeepRx Fully Convolutional Deep Learning Receiver.pdf
   价值：深度学习接收机代表工作；用于说明 neural receiver 已是成熟研究方向，本项目不能声称“首次提出神经接收机”。

2. Learning During Detection: Continual Learning for Neural OFDM Receivers via DMRS
   文件：Learning During Detection Continual Learning for Neural OFDM Receivers via DMRS.pdf
   价值：部署期间使用 DMRS/pilot 持续更新接收机；与本项目“在线期间按帧适配”最接近，但不是 PPO 控制 PEFT/块检测策略。

3. Online Learning of Modular Bayesian Deep Receivers: Single-Step Adaptation with Streaming Data
   文件：Online Learning of Modular Bayesian Deep Receivers Single-Step Adaptation.pdf
   价值：在线 Bayesian deep receiver / streaming adaptation 参考；适合作为非 RL 在线学习 baseline 背景。

4. P-FTNet: Pilot-Conditioned and Feature-Augmented Transformer Network for Equalization Under Extreme Delay Spread
   文件：P-FTNet Pilot-Conditioned and Feature-Augmented Transformer Network for Equalization Under Extreme Delay Spread.pdf
   价值：Pilot 条件、Transformer、极端 delay spread 下神经均衡背景。

二、RL / PPO / 动作控制

5. Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms
   文件：Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms.pdf
   价值：PPO 方法基础引用。

6. Composite Reward Design in PPO-Driven Adaptive Filtering
   文件：Composite Reward Design in PPO-Driven Adaptive Filtering.pdf
   价值：PPO 控制滤波器更新和复合 reward 设计参考。

7. Improved Channel Equalization using Deep Reinforcement Learning and Optimization
   文件：Improved Channel Equalization using Deep Reinforcement Learning and Optimization.pdf
   价值：DRL channel equalization 直接相关工作；用于说明 RL 均衡已有研究。

8. RIS-Enabled Wireless Channel Equalization: Adaptive RIS Equalizer and Deep Reinforcement Learning
   文件：RIS-Enabled Wireless Channel Equalization Adaptive RIS Equalizer and Deep Reinforcement Learning.pdf
   价值：RIS 场景 DRL equalization 工作；只作为相关工作边界，不引入 RIS 研发路线。

9. Cluster channel equalization using adaptive sensing and reinforcement learning for UAV communication
   文件：当前未下载 PDF；见 download_manifest.json
   DOI：10.7717/peerj-cs.2557
   价值：UAV 场景 RL/自适应 sensing 与信道均衡相关工作；当前命令行环境下载 PDF 返回 403。

三、EME / 极端长回波场景

10. Modeling and Characterization of Broadband Earth-Moon-Earth Communication Channels
    文件：Modeling and Characterization of Broadband Earth-Moon-Earth Communication Channels.pdf
    价值：EME 宽带信道建模与表征；用于说明 EME-inspired 极端稀疏长回波场景来源。

11. Frequency-Dependent Characteristics of the EME Path
    文件：Frequency-Dependent Characteristics of the EME Path.pdf
    价值：EME 路径频率相关特征背景；用于场景动机，不等同于完整物理 EME 仿真。

写作注意：
- 不能写“无人研究过神经均衡 / RL 均衡 / PPO / EME 信道建模”。
- 可以写：现有工作分别覆盖神经接收机、在线学习、RL equalization、PPO 控制和 EME 建模，但当前项目关注的组合是 Level B EME-inspired 极端稀疏长回波 + 整帧非因果块均衡 + Pilot/Reward 隔离 + Data-label isolation + 公平配对 baseline 矩阵。
- 修复 baseline 后的正式矩阵显示 PPO 已达到 BER_data < 0.01，但未优于 Best Fixed；论文结论必须按新矩阵结果表述。
