=== Reference Literature Summary ===
Topic: RL-based Online Adaptive Channel Equalization
Update: 2026-06-05, 6 PDFs total in reference/

=== Newly Downloaded Papers Analysis ===

[2] Obeed & Jian 2026 - 'Learning During Detection'
    arXiv:2602.20361 | Huawei Canada
    Method: DMRS-driven continual learning (SGD fine-tune, non-RL)
    Key: Redesign pilots as dual-purpose (demodulation + learning)
          3 pilot designs, 2 receiver architectures
    Result: Zero-overhead online adaptation, tracks fast/slow channels
    Gap: Uses SGD not RL, but closest online receiver framework

[3] Bereketoglu 2025 - 'Composite Reward PPO Adaptive Filtering'
    arXiv:2506.06323 | METU, IEEE SPL
    Method: PPO-driven adaptive filter (on-policy RL)  *** MOST RELEVANT ***
    Key: MDP(state=signal window+filter-state, action=filter-coeff-updates,
         reward=SNR_improvement - MSE - TV_smoothness)
    Actor-Critic: LSTM(128) actor + 2-layer MLP critic
    Result: Outperforms LMS/RLS/Wiener/Kalman on multiple noise types
    Gap: Signal denoising scenario, not symbol-level comms equalization

[4] Gusakov et al. 2025 - 'Modular Bayesian Deep Receivers'
    arXiv:2511.06045 | BGU/Northeastern
    Method: Variational Bayesian online learning (non-RL)
    Key: Single-step posterior update replaces multi-epoch SGD
          Modular DeepSIC architecture for parallelization
    Result: 1-2 order latency reduction, same BER as multi-epoch
    Gap: Not RL; needs gradient; Bayesian framework adds complexity

[5] Ben-Itzhak & Ayanoglu 2026 - 'RIS Equalizer DRL'
    arXiv:2603.02489 | UC Irvine, 40 pages
    Method: DDPG/TD3/SAC optimize RIS reflection coeffs for spatial equalization
    Key: State=pulse response y_t, Action=RIS phases Gamma (continuous),
         Reward=main-tap-energy - ISI-energy
    SAC best: fast convergence, no CSI needed, comparable to ARISE(SD)
    Gap: Not traditional equalizer (RIS spatial domain); DRL controls RIS not symbols

[6] Mo, Chang & Kan 2021 - 'DRL-MCTS MIMO Detection'
    arXiv:2102.00178 | Academia Sinica, IEEE TWC
    Method: DRL + Monte Carlo Tree Search for MIMO symbol detection
    Key: MDP formulation; Policy+State value networks guide MCTS
          Self-play training; avoids supervised labeling dilemma
    Result: Outperforms ZF/MMSE/DNN detectors, robust to channel variations
    Gap: Offline training; MCTS high latency; MIMO detection not equalization
